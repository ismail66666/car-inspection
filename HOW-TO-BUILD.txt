CAR INSPECTION - ANDROID APP
============================

This folder is the complete Android project. Pick ONE way to turn it into an APK.

OPTION A - On your PC with Android Studio (free)
  1. Install Android Studio from developer.android.com/studio (accept the defaults).
  2. Open Android Studio > Open > choose this CarInspection-Android folder.
     The first time, it downloads what it needs. Wait until the bottom bar says it's done.
  3. Menu: Build > Generate App Bundles or APKs > Generate APKs.
  4. When it finishes, click "locate". The APK is in:
       app\build\outputs\apk\debug\app-debug.apk

OPTION B - In the cloud with GitHub (free, nothing to install)
  1. Make a free account at github.com and create a new repository.
  2. Upload everything in this folder (keep the folders as they are,
     including the hidden .github folder).
  3. Open the "Actions" tab. The "Build APK" job starts by itself
     (or press "Run workflow").
  4. When it shows a green check, open it and download "CarInspection-apk".
     Unzip it to get the APK.

INSTALLING ON A PHONE
  - Send the APK to the phone (email, WhatsApp, Google Drive, USB cable).
  - Tap it. Android asks to allow installing from this source: allow it, then Install.
  - The same APK works on any Android 7.0 or newer phone, so you can share it with your techs.

UPDATING THE APP LATER
  - The inspection screen is app\src\main\assets\index.html.
  - After changing anything, build again and install the new APK over the old one.
    Inspections saved on the phone are kept.
